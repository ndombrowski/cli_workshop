Bring: your own laptop (Windows, Mac or Linux). No coding experience needed.

**Description**: More and more biological research produces data that is too big for a spreadsheet and too slow for a laptop. Sequencing data is a typical example, and many of the tools to analyse it only run on the command line. This hands-on workshop is a first step into that world, for biologists who have never typed a command before. This workshop runs for two days, and you will learn the following:

1. On day 1 you work on your own laptop. You learn how to find your way around folders, download and unpack data, look inside large sequence files, and count and search through them with a few simple commands. You also learn how to repeat a step for many files at once and how to save your commands in a script.
2. On day 2 we move to Crunchomics, the UvA high-performance computer. You learn how to log in, copy your data there, install your own software and send analysis jobs to the cluster. As a worked example, we check and clean sequencing reads with SeqKit and fastp.

Throughout both days you keep notes of every command you run, so that your analysis can be repeated and understood later, by you or by others.

Requirements: before the workshop, please (1) request access to Crunchomics and (2) set up a terminal on your laptop and test your login. The attached instructions take about 30 to 60 minutes. Requesting access can take a few days, so please start early. If something does not work, contact Nina Dombrowski (n.dombrowski@uva.nl) at the latest one week before the workshop.
